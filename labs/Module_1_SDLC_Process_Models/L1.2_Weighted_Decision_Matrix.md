## 2. Conclusion Supporting Spiral Model (5 Lines)
1. **Spiral achieves the highest weighted score (4.45/5.00)** because it dedicates an explicit risk-analysis quadrant in every iteration to resolve GPS signal loss inside tunnels before full fleet rollout.
2. Unlike Waterfall or V-Model, Spiral lets the team build and calibrate the **Arrival Time Estimator prototype** early on simulated tunnel segments before scaling to all **600 buses**.
3. Each Spiral cycle incrementally integrates high-risk modules—starting with live GPS telemetry, followed by tunnel dead-reckoning, and finally the **driver duty roster** engine.
4. Continuous risk re-evaluation prevents costly post-deployment failures when real-world tunnel congestion or cold-start GPS delays occur across 600 active vehicles.
5. Although Prototyping scores well on UI feedback (3.90), Spiral combines rapid prototyping with rigorous engineering risk control and scalability testing required for a city-wide transport corporation.
